How I work

Four steps, in this order

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Sessions with the people who own the work. I map what actually happens, not what the process document says.

  2. 02

    Make flow visible

    One board and a few metrics everyone can read: cycle time, throughput, work in progress.

  3. 03

    Stabilise

    Limit work in progress, fix the worst handoffs, automate the status relay.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Your team owns the system. I leave the cadences, the dashboards and the documentation behind.
